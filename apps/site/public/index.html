Satark

Reads what you never read. Satark is a free Chrome side-panel extension that checks scam pages and messages, terms and conditions, and loan or rent agreements, and explains the risk in plain words with the exact quote as evidence.

Private by design

Satark runs Gemini Nano on your laptop when it can. When it cannot (older laptops, Hindi pages, very long documents), it removes personal details first, asks you, and then uses Gemini in the cloud through Firebase AI Logic. After every check it shows a Privacy Receipt: where it ran, why, and how much left the device.

Install

  1. Download the latest ZIP from the GitHub releases page and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick the unzipped folder.
  3. Pin the Satark icon and click it on any page.

Needs desktop Chrome 139 or later. "Cloud only" mode works on any laptop.
